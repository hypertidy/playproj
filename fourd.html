Click the globe for every layer's value there on this date, its cell and chunk, and the profile against depth for an ocean layer.

A browser can read a chunk only if the host of the original file allows reads from other sites (CORS). The table counts reads by host as layers load; the button reads one chunk of every dataset, and links each one it reads to gridlook and to the store explorer.

How it works

Every layer is an array with a time axis and, for an ocean model, a depth axis. The clock is a date, and each layer finds its own nearest time step, so daily sea ice from 1978, daily SST from 1981 and a daily ocean model from 1993 line up on one day; a layer with nothing near that date says so.

The datasets are virtual stores: byte-reference indexes on source.coop that point into the original netCDF files where they are published. The page reads each one's flat/arrays.json for its axes and units, opens its Icechunk form with icechunk-js, and reads only the chunks it draws with zarrita.js. Where the index lives is named once, in data/fourd/stores.json.

Depth is a place, not a slider: for an ocean layer the page reads every level down to a chosen depth over a box, and draws one level as a shell below the surface, sunk by its depth times the exaggeration, and two sections through the box along a meridian and a parallel. Decoded windows are cached, and the next few dates are read ahead while one is shown, so Play only shows dates that have arrived.

The globe unfolds onto a map: equidistant from a centre (the box, or a picked point), or Spilhaus, which puts the world ocean in one square. Depth stays depth: on the map it goes down from the plane by the same exaggeration. The horizon keeps only what lies within a range of the centre, on the globe and on the map.

Each layer can be drawn as its colour fill, as contour lines, or both: lines are traced through the cell centres (marching squares) at the layer's contour step, on surfaces and on the shell and sections of an ocean layer, and a sea ice layer also gets its edge at 15 percent. The links beside a layer open the same array and date in gridlook or the store explorer.
