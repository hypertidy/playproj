Section: ice white, water blue, rock brown; the white line is the ice surface, the pale blue line the base of floating ice, orange REMA, dashed cyan GEBCO 2026, the yellow band the bed plus or minus its uncertainty, pink lines where the section crosses a grounding line.

How it works

Bedmap3 grids ice thickness, bed and surface elevation over Antarctica at 500 m, with a mask of grounded ice, floating ice shelves and rock. Each array is a cloud-optimised GeoTIFF on source.coop with overviews, so the page reads only the window round the line and block, at the level whose pixels suit its length: a few requests for a line across the continent as for one across a glacier.

Each sample becomes three bands: rock up to the bed, water from the bed to the ice base (under a floating shelf) or to sea level (the open sea), and ice from its base to its surface. Grounded ice rests on its bed; floating ice has its base at its surface less its thickness. The cut face of the block is the section itself, the other three faces are the same bands along the block's edges, and its top surfaces are the bed, the ice and the sea from the same grid. With the block on both sides of the line, the ice is taken off the right-hand side to show the bed under it (unflooded and not rebounded), and the block can be bent over the Earth's curve, for a line across the continent.

A drill site is a red rod from the bed up through the surface, with the ice thickness, bed and their uncertainties read there at Bedmap3's full 500 m.

REMA gives heights above the WGS84 ellipsoid; the page subtracts the EGM2008 geoid (as the Geoid page does) so its line sits with Bedmap3's and GEBCO's heights above sea level. Where REMA and Bedmap3's surface part, that is the datum, the date and the resolution of each source, and worth a look. GEBCO 2026 is the ice surface in Antarctica and the sea floor beyond, in longitude and latitude, sampled through the projection.

  • Bedmap3: Pritchard, H.D., Fretwell, P.T., Fremand, A.C., et al. (2025) Bedmap3 updated ice bed, surface and thickness gridded datasets for Antarctica. Sci Data 12, 414; data from the UK Polar Data Centre (CC BY 4.0), copied to source.coop.
  • REMA v2: Howat, I. et al., Polar Geospatial Center; the 100 m, 500 m and 1 km mosaics from rema-ovr.
  • GEBCO Compilation Group (2026) GEBCO 2026 Grid, on source.coop.
