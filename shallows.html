What is being solved

Depth h and momentum (hu, hv) under the shallow water equations, on the grid. Fluxes use the HLL Riemann solver with hydrostatic reconstruction (Audusse et al. 2004), so water at rest stays at rest and depth stays non-negative as cells wet and dry. Order 2 adds MUSCL (minmod) reconstruction and Heun time stepping. Manning friction is semi-implicit; Earth's rotation is an exact turn of the momentum each step (Strang split).

Generated terrain uses the same generator as the R package (sw_generate): the same shape, size and seed give the same ground in R and here, to 1e-13 m. Storm Bay was extracted in R with sw_extract, which fills a local UTM grid from the Tasmania 2 m DEM, Copernicus GLO-30 and GEBCO in that order.

The budget compares water stored on the grid with water in (start, rain, clicks) minus water out through the edges; it should stay near 1e-12.
