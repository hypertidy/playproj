What is being solved

Depth h and momentum (hu, hv) under the shallow water equations, on the raster grid. Fluxes use the HLL Riemann solver with hydrostatic reconstruction (Audusse et al. 2004), so water at rest stays at rest and depth stays non-negative as cells wet and dry. Order 2 adds MUSCL (minmod) reconstruction and Heun time stepping. The step size obeys the CFL condition plus a bed-slope acceleration limit. Friction is Manning, applied semi-implicitly.

The budget compares water stored on the grid with water in (initial, rain, clicks) minus water out through the edges; it should stay near 1e-12. The benchmarks show the analytic solution as a line beside the model.

This page runs src/core/shallows.js, a port of the R package's C++ kernel (playproj shallows/). On the same inputs the two agree to 1e-12 or better.
