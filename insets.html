    How it works

    An inset is a second map on the same sheet. It has its own extent, scale and position, and often its own projection. A projection trades shape against area against distance. An inset trades true position, relative size and a continuous surface against using the page well. On this page every piece of a map is drawn the same way: project it, then place it with an affine, page = at + s R(rot) (F(lon, lat) - F(anchor)). A piece in its true place uses the main projection with s = 1. An inset uses its own projection and gets a box, a scale bar and a locator line. A moved piece (sp::elide in R, or the move-scale-rotate recipes for Alaska and Hawaii) is the same arithmetic with nothing on the page to admit it.

    The table gives the sheet (the box around every piece and frame), the share of it that is land or map, and how much of the sheet's width or height the main piece gets: a page of a fixed size draws the main piece at that share of the size it would have on its own. For each piece it gives the scale it is drawn at against the main frame, its area as drawn against its true area (at the main frame's scale), and the largest angle the projection it is drawn in bends anywhere over it. The distance readout measures two places on the page at the main frame's scale. Across a box that number means nothing. On a moved piece it means something wrong.

    GS50 is John P. Snyder's modified stereographic for the 50 states (USGS, 1982). It is a tenth-order complex polynomial applied to an oblique stereographic on Clarke 1866. Its scale varies by less than 2 percent over all 50 states, but it holds nowhere else. It is ported here from PROJ's mod_ster.cpp, as is Snyder's modified stereographic for Alaska. geoAlbersUsa is rebuilt from d3-geo's source: three spherical Albers, with Alaska at 0.35 and Hawaii at 1, placed by translate offsets in units of the scale. Both are checked to a nanometre and a picometre against PROJ and d3-geo in test/check-inset.mjs.

    Hash keys: s (step), scene, main, aks (Alaska's scale in the composite), tp (all in true place), L (layout: key:mode,s,rot,x km,y km joined by ;, with mode t true, i inset, m moved, o off), outline, leader, ctx, grat, names, edit, v (view centre and width in km).
